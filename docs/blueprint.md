# **App Name**: SabziNow

## Core Features:

- Restaurant/Business Listing: Display a list of restaurants and businesses that are registered on the platform.
- Business Registration: Enable businesses to register themselves on the platform to list their offerings.
- Business Dashboard: Provide a dashboard for restaurants/businesses to manage their listings and track their ad status.
- Cash on Delivery: Support cash on delivery as the only payment method.
- Sponsored Listings: Show sponsored listings at the top of the search results and on the home page for businesses with active ads.
- Ad Subscription via WhatsApp: Upon clicking an add subscription link, redirect users to WhatsApp with pre-filled message (AbdullahPremium code) for ad subscription requests.
- Ad Activation Tool: The 'AbdullahPremium' code, when entered, will be verified and activated through a tool which then enables their ad for 1 month.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Fresh green (#6AB04C) to represent freshness and vitality, in line with the food theme.
- Background color: Light green (#E5F2E0) for a clean and inviting look.
- Accent color: Yellow-Green (#A3C459) for highlighting interactive elements.
- Headline font: 'Poppins', a geometric sans-serif for a modern and fashionable appearance.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif for a warm and readable text.
- Use simple, outline-style icons that are easy to understand and consistent with the green theme.
- A grid-based layout that is clean and easy to navigate.
